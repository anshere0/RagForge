Apex Institute of Technology & Science (Apex University) — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What courses and degree programs are offered at Apex University?
Apex University offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degree programs across multiple disciplines:
- Engineering & Technology: B.Tech in Computer Science (CSE), AI & Data Science, Electronics & Communication (ECE), Mechanical, and Civil Engineering; M.Tech; Ph.D.
- Computer Applications: BCA, MCA.
- Management: BBA, MBA (Specializations in Finance, Marketing, HR, and Business Analytics).
- Basic & Applied Sciences: B.Sc, M.Sc in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biotechnology.

3. What are the eligibility criteria and admission requirements for B.Tech CSE?
For B.Tech admission, candidates must have passed 10+2 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) with a minimum aggregate score of 60% (55% for SC/ST candidates). Admission is based on ranks in JEE Main, KCET, or the Apex University Entrance Exam (AUEE).

4. What is the fee structure for B.Tech, BCA, and MBA courses?
- B.Tech (All Branches): ₹1,80,000 per academic year (Tuition fee: ₹1,50,000 + Exam & Lab fee: ₹30,000).
- BCA: ₹95,000 per academic year.
- MCA: ₹1,20,000 per academic year.
- MBA: ₹2,20,000 per academic year.
- Hostel & Mess Charges: ₹85,000 per year (Non-AC Sharing) / ₹1,25,000 per year (AC Double Sharing with food).

5. What scholarships are available for meritorious and needy students?
Apex University offers merit-based and category-based scholarship assistance:
- Merit Scholarship: 50% tuition fee waiver for students scoring >95% in 10+2 / JEE Main percentile >98.
- Dean's List Scholarship: 25% tuition fee waiver for students scoring between 85% - 94.9% in 10+2.
- Sports & Defense Merit: 20% waiver for state/national level athletes and children of armed forces personnel.
- Government Financial Assistance: Full assistance provided for Post-Matric SC/ST/OBC national scholarships.

7. What is the campus placement record and top recruiter list?
Apex University maintains a 94% placement rate for the 2025 graduating batch:
- Highest International Package: ₹48 LPA (offered by Microsoft)
- Highest Domestic Package: ₹32 LPA (offered by Amazon)
- Average CTC across B.Tech CSE: ₹8.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, Amazon, Microsoft, IBM, Deloitte, Tata Motors, and L&T Technology Services.

8. What is the attendance policy and minimum criteria for appearing in semester exams?
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all theory lectures and practical labs to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Medical leave up to 10% can be condoned by the Dean of Academic Affairs upon submitting valid medical certificates within 3 days of returning.

9. What hostel and dining facilities are provided on campus?
Apex University provides separate in-campus hostels for boys and girls with 24x7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, biometric entry, study lounges, indoor gym, and laundry facilities. The central student dining hall serves 4 hygienic meals daily (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner) offering both North Indian and South Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian options.

10. How can I apply online for admission?
You can apply online by visiting https://apexuniversity.edu.in/apply, filling out the digital registration form, uploading scanned copies of your 10th & 12th marksheets, and paying the application fee of ₹1,000 via Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Net Banking.
